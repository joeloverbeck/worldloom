## Problem

At intake, the `emergent-pressure-events` skill (created 2026-04-30; auto-generated by `skill-creator` from `archive/brainstorming/emergent-pressure-events.md`) explicitly used `task_type='other'` in its `mcp__worldloom__get_context_packet` calls and documented the deferral in its own Guardrails:

> **Deferred MCPENH ticket: register `task_type='emergent_pressure_events'` profile.** Currently uses `task_type='other'` per the design decision. The generic profile is sufficient for Phase 1 pressure inventory and Phase 6 Canon Safety Check but produces a slightly broader / less prioritized packet load. A future MCPENH ticket should register an EPE-specific profile prioritizing: hard_canon CFs, every INV record, every M record (full firewall surface), section records for institutions / economy-and-resources / magic-or-tech-systems / peoples-and-species, recent CH records since the prior batch's date.

This was a documented suboptimality that the skill rewrite could not fix on its own — it required extending the MCP enum + ranking-profile registry. EPE's retrieval shape is distinct from the four MCPENH-002 task_types: the skill enumerates currently-active world pressures (Phase 1 Pressure Inventory) by scanning hard_canon CFs whose `costs_and_limits` / `visible_consequences` describe ongoing strain, plus institution/economy/magic-or-tech-systems/peoples-and-species section records describing contestation, plus active mystery-reserve entries, plus recent change-log entries since the prior EPE batch's date. Before this ticket, the `'other'` fallback applied a generic baseline that gave equal weight to records EPE did not need (e.g., timeline-Layer SEC records, geography SEC records when no climate pressure is in play) while under-weighting the records EPE specifically anchors against (recent CH entries, hard_canon CFs whose distribution implies strain).

Before this ticket landed, every EPE invocation paid a slightly broader / less prioritized packet load on every Pre-flight, and the skill carried an explicit Guardrails note pointing at this deferred work.
