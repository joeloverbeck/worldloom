# Manual Story Studio Prompt Example

This report summarizes the live prompt-generation path in Manual Story Studio and gives one complete example prompt shape for an external LLM prose pass. The story values below are made up. The two long document bodies are represented as placeholders because the real composer inserts those files verbatim at compose time:

- `docs/prose-renderer-contract/content-policy.md` becomes section 1.
- `docs/manual-story-studio/prose-craft-contract.md` becomes section 13.

## Generation Path

The prompt is generated by `tools/manual-story-studio/src/prompt/compose.ts`. `composePrompt()` validates a non-empty moment directive, loads `manual-story.yaml`, loads the optional prompt working set, resolves current cast and selected records, loads an optional beat template, reads the content policy and prose-craft contract from disk, builds a translator context, optionally loads the last paragraph of the latest accepted segment, assembles the markdown, and then lints it before copy/save.

The final markdown is assembled by `tools/manual-story-studio/src/prompt/sections/index.ts`. That file owns the fixed 15-section order and headings. Individual section emitters render either fixed prose, metadata, the selected moment directive, or translated record content.

Record-shaped material is not dumped as raw YAML. It is translated by the per-class translators under `tools/manual-story-studio/src/prompt/translators/`. For example, cast records become profile blocks, emotions become short state bullets, relationships become axis summaries, plans and intentions become active-purpose bullets, secrets carry audience guidance, and physical continuity gathers locations, bodies, objects, and concrete facts.

## Example Prompt

```markdown
## 1. Content Policy

## 2. Story Contract

**Title:** The Salt Clock
**Premise:** A disgraced tide engineer and a palace courier must decide whether to expose the harbor sabotage before the city loses its freshwater gates.
**Tone:** Intimate, pressure-heavy, civic dread under polite surfaces
**Point of view:** close third, Mara
**Tense:** past
**Content intensity:** mature
**Explicitness:** implied unless central
**Language register:** literary, concrete, low ornament

**Prose preferences:**
- Psychic distance: close
- Dialogue density: moment-led
- Interiority: free indirect
- Paragraphing: literary

## 3. Current Situation

**Author's current handoff:**

Mara has reached the abandoned clock house beneath the west sluice after following Aster's coded delivery route. Aster arrived first and has been waiting with the broken brass seal in his palm. Neither of them has said aloud that the seal belongs to the Minister's private water office. Rain is starting above them; if the tide turns before the west gate is locked, the lower wards will flood with brine.

**Most recent prose (last paragraph):**

The clock house answered the first rain with a hollow ticking that did not come from gears. Mara stopped on the final stair, one hand on the wet rail, and saw Aster standing where the old tide dial should have been.

## 4. Manual Moment Directive

Write the next exchange in which Mara asks Aster where he got the broken seal, Aster tries to warn her off without lying, and Mara realizes he is protecting someone inside the water office.

## 5. Required Beat Cluster

Render only the next 3-5 beats as continuous prose. Begin from the current situation. Follow the manual moment directive. Stop as soon as the immediate exchange or action produces the first materially new response point: a decision pressure, emotional turn, information change, practical result, refusal, reveal-withheld, changed tactic, or newly exposed vulnerability. Do not summarize future consequences. Do not add choices. Do not add headings. Do not explain the prose.

## 6. Optional Beat Template Guidance

- **pressure_open**: Begin with a concrete sensory pressure from the clock house before dialogue starts.
- **evasive_answer**: Let Aster answer with a technically true statement that avoids the central question.
- **recognition_turn**: Let Mara infer the protection motive from what Aster refuses to name.
- **stop_on_pressure**: End when Mara has a new decision pressure, not after she acts on it.

## 7. Cast and Voice

**POV:** Mara Venn

### Mara Venn

**Identity**

**One line:** Former municipal tide engineer, dismissed after refusing to sign off on a falsified gate-safety report.

**Public face:** Precise, useful, visibly tired; treats procedure as a moral language.

**Private pressure:** She wants proof that her disgrace was not only personal failure, but she fears needing the truth more than the city needs her.

**World pressure core**

**World-produced wound:** The city made her responsible for water safety, then punished her for reporting that the safety numbers were false.

**Active appetite:** To recover enough authority to stop the west gate from being opened on a compromised tide.

**Self-mythology:** If she can name the fault accurately enough, someone honest will have to act.

**Irreconcilable contradiction:** She distrusts institutions but still speaks in institutional procedures when frightened.

**Relational charge:** Aster's access to palace routes makes him both useful and dangerous.

**Moral/psychological edge:** She can be cruel when someone wastes time with elegance.

**Cannot be swapped out because:** The scene depends on someone who understands tide mechanics, civic liability, and the cost of being made disposable.

**Voice**

**Baseline:** Short declarative sentences; practical nouns; rarely asks a question she has not already narrowed.

**Under pressure:** Becomes colder and more exact, as if precision can keep panic out.

**Intimacy:** Lets one plain sentence do the work.

**Evasion:** Redirects to measurements, logs, and mechanisms.

**Anger:** Quiet, procedural, almost clerical.

**Lying:** Omits the emotional reason, not the factual one.

*Anti-generic warnings:*
- Do not make her quippy.
- Do not make her explain her competence to herself.

**Body and presence**

**Physicality:** Narrow-shouldered, steady-handed, with the stillness of someone trained near dangerous machinery.

**Body limits:** Left knee stiffens on stairs after old floodwall injury.

**Habitual gestures:** Counts under her breath when measuring intervals; touches metal before trusting it.

**Clothing/presentation:** Faded municipal coat with the insignia removed, oil-dark cuffs, practical boots.

**Social presentation:** Polite enough to be admitted, blunt enough to be remembered.

**Pressure behavior**

**Cornered:** Asks for exact names and times.

**Tempted:** Accepts risk if it produces a testable fact.

**Humiliated:** Goes formal.

**Protecting attachment:** Stands between the person and the mechanism, then pretends it was positional necessity.

**Offered power:** Asks who pays for it.

**Perception and embodiment**

**Notices:** Water marks, tool wear, mismatched metal, official phrasing.

**Misses:** Performative charm when it does not alter the mechanism.

**Misreads:** Silence as contempt before fear.

**Sensory bias:** Hears rhythm and pressure before faces.

**Agency and planning**

**Default strategy:** Isolate the mechanism, identify the failure point, force a responsible office to sign its name.

**Risk style:** Accepts physical danger more readily than social exposure.

**Fallback style:** Writes down facts when she cannot force action.

**Planning blind spots:** Underestimates people acting from loyalty rather than interest.

**Relationships**

- *Toward Aster Vale:* Treats him as a sealed conduit: valuable if opened, suspect if polished.

**Prose constraints**

*Prose must not imply:*
- Mara has forgiven the city.

*Forbidden inventions:*
- A surviving parent who can intervene.

*Voice -- do not do:*
- breezy banter

### Aster Vale

**Identity**

**One line:** Palace courier with access to private offices and a practiced talent for making urgency look like courtesy.

**Public face:** Elegant, deferential, impossible to hurry from the outside.

**Private pressure:** He has already chosen a side but wants to avoid naming the person who forced that choice.

**World pressure core**

**World-produced wound:** Palace service taught him that truth is safest when carried as a sealed object.

**Active appetite:** To keep Mara from walking into the Minister's trap before he can move the proof.

**Self-mythology:** He is only a messenger, so the moral burden belongs elsewhere.

**Irreconcilable contradiction:** He keeps choosing when he insists he is only delivering.

**Relational charge:** Mara sees through the shape of his omissions faster than most officials see through lies.

**Moral/psychological edge:** He will sacrifice his own credibility before exposing his sister.

**Cannot be swapped out because:** The moment requires someone with palace access, courier discipline, and a personal reason to conceal the source.

**Voice**

**Baseline:** Courteous, elliptical, shaped by palace service.

**Under pressure:** Gives exact but incomplete answers.

**Intimacy:** Drops titles first, then formality.

**Evasion:** Offers logistics instead of motive.

**Anger:** Becomes very still and very polite.

**Lying:** Avoids direct falsehood if an omission will survive.

*Anti-generic warnings:*
- Do not make him flirt to deflect serious risk.
- Do not make him smug.

**Body and presence**

**Physicality:** Lean, careful, used to moving quietly through guarded corridors.

**Body limits:** Right wrist is recently bruised under the glove.

**Habitual gestures:** Smooths the edge of paper before speaking.

**Clothing/presentation:** Dark courier coat, rain-dulled silver clasp, one glove damp.

**Social presentation:** Makes deference feel like a closed door.

**Pressure behavior**

**Cornered:** Gives one useful truth and keeps the dangerous name back.

**Tempted:** Wants to trust competence more than authority.

**Humiliated:** Retreats into titles.

**Protecting attachment:** Places himself between inquiry and the protected person.

**Offered power:** Distrusts anyone who offers it quickly.

**Perception and embodiment**

**Notices:** Who can overhear, who signed a seal, who is not where duty says they should be.

**Misses:** The mechanical consequences of small delays.

**Misreads:** Mara's coldness as lack of fear.

**Sensory bias:** Registers footsteps, paper, breath, and doors.

**Agency and planning**

**Default strategy:** Move the object, delay the question, preserve deniability.

**Risk style:** Accepts reputational risk before endangering family.

**Fallback style:** Turns a confession into an instruction.

**Planning blind spots:** Underestimates how fast Mara can infer structure from fragments.

**Relationships**

- *Toward Mara Venn:* Respects her precision, fears her directness, and needs her to remain outside the palace's prepared accusation.

**Prose constraints**

*Prose must not imply:*
- Aster enjoys deceiving Mara.

*Forbidden inventions:*
- magical courier abilities

*Voice -- do not do:*
- theatrical villain polish

## 8. Emotional and Relationship State

**Emotions:**
- Mara Venn feels strongly fraught: She is angry enough to be precise and afraid that Aster's answer will prove the sabotage is inside the office she once served.
- Aster Vale feels moderately mixed: He wants Mara to understand the danger without making him say his sister's name.

**Relationships:**

### Mara Venn <-> Aster Vale

Axes: trust: low; fear: medium; respect: high; familiarity: low

Dominant axis: respect under suspicion

Current state: They are not allies yet. Mara trusts Aster's access more than his motives; Aster trusts Mara's competence more than her restraint.

## 9. Current Intentions and Plans

**Intentions:**
- Mara Venn intends: Force Aster to identify how the seal left the Minister's private water office without letting him redirect the question. (regarding Broken water-office seal)
- Aster Vale intends: Warn Mara away from the west-gate inquiry while preserving the protected source. (regarding Minister's private water office)

**Plans:**
- Plan (Aster Vale): Move the original ledger page to the bell tower cache before the tide office can inventory missing documents.
  Target/object: Ledger page from the west-gate audit
  Visibility: private -- held internally

## 10. Relevant Beliefs, Secrets, and Open Questions

**Beliefs:**
- Mara Venn thinks: Aster is protecting someone with access to official seals (partly true; partly mistaken). Confidence: strong.

**Secrets:**
- Secret: Aster's sister copied the west-gate audit page and used his courier satchel to get it out of the office.
  Held by: Aster Vale
  Audience: Known only to the listed holders; do not disclose to other characters
  Forbidden reveals: sister copied the audit page, sister works in the private water office

**Open questions:**
- Open question: Who ordered the west gate opened on a bad tide?
  Context: The official order exists, but the signature chain is not yet established.
  Detail: The prose may sharpen suspicion but must not answer the question in this segment.
  Answer status: the author knows the answer.
  Reveal: Do not let the prose resolve this question unless: Mara obtains the ledger page; Aster names the source

**Must not reveal:**
- Aster's sister copied the west-gate audit page and used his courier satchel to get it out of the office.

## 11. Physical Continuity

**Locations:**

### Abandoned west-sluice clock house

Summary: A below-street maintenance chamber with a dead tide dial, narrow stairs, brass fittings gone green, and one dry alcove where couriers used to wait out storms.

Details: Rainwater ticks through the ceiling in uneven intervals. The west gate's pressure chain can be heard through the wall when the tide begins to turn.

**Bodies (involved cast):**
- Mara Venn: Narrow-shouldered, steady-handed, with the stillness of someone trained near dangerous machinery.; limits: Left knee stiffens on stairs after old floodwall injury.; gestures: Counts under her breath when measuring intervals; touches metal before trusting it.; presentation: Faded municipal coat with the insignia removed, oil-dark cuffs, practical boots.
- Aster Vale: Lean, careful, used to moving quietly through guarded corridors.; limits: Right wrist is recently bruised under the glove.; gestures: Smooths the edge of paper before speaking.; presentation: Dark courier coat, rain-dulled silver clasp, one glove damp.

**Objects/props:**
- Broken water-office seal: A snapped brass seal from the Minister's private water office, still threaded with blue wax cord.
  Current holder: Aster Vale
  Current location: Abandoned west-sluice clock house
  Details: The break is fresh. Mara can identify the office by the stamp even though one edge is crushed.

**Recent concrete facts:**
- Tide turn is imminent: The west gate will take brine pressure within minutes once the rainwater surge reaches the lower channel.

- Do not let the prose imply: Aster enjoys deceiving Mara.

## 14. Stop Rule

Write only the beat cluster requested. Let meaningful action, emotional movement, relational pressure, practical consequence, or discovery occur if the directive calls for it. Stop when the segment reaches the first new decision point, response point, interruption, irreversible beat, or changed immediate pressure that would require the author to choose what happens next. Do not summarize downstream aftermath. Do not continue into the next exchange merely to resolve tension. Do not declare durable continuity changes outside the prose. The author will update story records manually after accepting or rejecting this segment.

## 15. Output Instruction

Output prose only. No commentary. No Markdown headings. No bullet points. No notes. Do not use the words 'page', 'scene', 'act', 'arc', 'midpoint', 'climax', or any other narrative-structure language.
```
