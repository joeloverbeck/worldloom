# Phase 9: Validation Gates (Canon Safety Check phase)

Defense-in-depth checks before Phase 11 write. Phase 9 has 19 gates. Each gate must record PASS, FAIL, or DEFERRED with a one-line rationale on the new page's `validation_trace` field (with the three deferred-trace keys recorded on `PG.deferred_validation_trace` instead — see below). A bare "PASS" without rationale is treated as FAIL per the FOUNDATIONS skill discipline. Any FAIL halts Phase 11 and routes to the responsible phase.

Three gates are split between plan-commit (this skill) and prose-finalize (`branching-story-page-prose-finalize`). The plan-time gates record on `PG.validation_trace`; the prose-coupled gates record on `PG.deferred_validation_trace` as `DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` at plan-commit and are flipped to PASS/FAIL by finalize Phase 5.

## Deferred validation trace (PG.deferred_validation_trace)

In addition to the 19 gates above, the PG record carries a separate `deferred_validation_trace` field with three keys, all recorded as `DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` at plan-commit and flipped to PASS/FAIL by `branching-story-page-prose-finalize` Phase 5:

- `prose_ledger_consistency: DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` (mirrors gate 7 above; the keys are duplicated across `validation_trace` and `deferred_validation_trace` for audit-trail clarity).
- `arc_trace_evidence_alignment: DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` (mirrors gate 15 above).
- `prose_critic_8_axis: DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` (the 8-axis prose critic verdict from finalize Phase 3; not a Phase 9 gate per se because the critic is a finalize-owned LLM-critic verdict rather than a structural validator, but it is recorded as a third deferred-trace key alongside the two deferred gates for symmetry with the bootstrap deferred-trace contract).

---

**Whole-class loads from Pre-flight power gates 1, 2, and 3**: M-record full bodies for gate 1's `forbidden`-status check + `M_resolution_claims` interrogation; INV-record full bodies for gate 2's `break_conditions` audit; the cross-record reachability set anchored at the full page-record root (`state_snapshot`, `storylet_realized`, `applied_event_ops`, and `emitted_choices`) for gate 3's recursive closure scan.

**Gates 3, 4, and 10 — operator-asserted rationale + structurally-enforced backstop**: gates 3 (recursive reference closure), 4 (snapshot-replay equality), and 10 (state_snapshot integrity) are operator-asserted at the page-cycle skill level — the operator inspects, asserts PASS with a one-line rationale on the new page's `validation_trace` field, and proceeds. They are ALSO structurally enforced at submit-time by the patch-engine pre-apply structural validators `recursive_reference_closure`, `snapshot_replay_equality`, and `state_snapshot_integrity` — all three landed and registered in `tools/validators/src/public/registry.ts` alongside `cross_file_reference`, `id_uniqueness`, `record_schema_compliance`, and the other structural validators. The validation_trace rationale remains as audit-trail documentation; the structural validator is the programmatic backstop. A drift between the two surfaces (operator asserts PASS but the snapshot/closure/integrity actually drifted) is caught by the validator at submit-time, so the operator-asserted PASS is no longer sole-defense — the structural-validator FAIL preempts the submit.

**Gates 13-17 — scene-commitment arc validators**: these gates are the Phase 9 surfaces introduced by SPEC-20. `effect_model_replay_safety`, `narrative_point_classification`, and `choice_worthiness_completeness` run at plan-commit; `arc_envelope_conformance` runs at plan-commit over the plan's frontmatter + inlined arc record (Layer 1 only); `arc_trace_evidence_alignment` defers to finalize Phase 5 because Layer 2 / Layer 3 ARC_TRACE extraction requires rendered prose.

**Gates 18-19 — plan-authoring backstops**: these gates are defense-in-depth backstops to Phase 7's post-LLM checks. Phase 7 routes a missing-section / unresolvable-id / malformed-frontmatter failure or cast Material Reality contradiction as a re-prompt within its own 3-cycle budget; if Phase 7 exhausts the budget and still escalates to Phase 10, Phase 9 gates 18-19 re-fire the checks and route back. The two-layered enforcement matters because the plan IS the prompt — corrupt or physically contradictory plans corrupt the rendered prose silently.

**DEFERRED gates 7 + 15**: at plan-commit there is no rendered prose to ledger-check (gate 7) and no ARC_TRACE to align with the arc's evidence spans (gate 15). Both gates are recorded as `DEFERRED — awaiting prose render` on `PG.deferred_validation_trace` and re-evaluated by `branching-story-page-prose-finalize` Phase 5 after the user supplies `pages-prose/PG-NNNN.md`.
