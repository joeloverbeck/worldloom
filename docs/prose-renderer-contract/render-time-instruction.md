# Render-Time Instruction - scene-plan canonical source

This is the canonical source for the render-time instruction block inlined verbatim into every `scene-prose-plans/SCN-<integer>.md` scene plan. `branching-story-scene-plan` inlines this file's body byte-for-byte. The block is the LLM-facing instruction the external renderer reads after the plan body; it covers scene-range rendering over committed `PG` records.

Do not edit this file lightly. Any change here propagates to every newly-authored scene plan; existing plans on disk retain the bytes they were written with.

---
```
INSTRUCTION:

Render the scene described by the plan above as continuous prose.

Honor the POV named in the plan, especially §1 Story Kernel Excerpt and any §17
Style and Register Notes. When the plan commits to a specific POV —
first-person, third-person close, alternating, or any other treatment — render
in that POV throughout; otherwise apply the Prose Craft Contract's Rule 1
defaults.

Render the selected scene range as one continuous prose unit. The beats in the
scene plan are the structural sketch — embody them as scene movement, NOT as a
beat-headered enumeration. Do not emit beat headers (no
`# Beat 1`, no `## Beat 2`, no equivalent section-heading enumeration). Drive
the prose toward the stopping point declared by the scene plan, and make the
consequences of the selected events legible as lived scene action rather than
ledger jargon.

Length follows content. Use the scene plan's beats and stopping point with the
Prose Craft Contract's Rule 11: render the scene only as long as the beat
movement, cast reactions, and natural next-decision point require. No padding to
fill space; no truncation to fit a budget; no words-per-scene target, minimum,
or maximum.

Render through what happens — what characters do, say, perceive, and attend to.
Use the Prose Craft Contract's Rule 7 to choose the scene's modality mix from the
beat and the scene's natural shape. Do not narrate meaning, summarize reactions,
label subtext, or deploy action, dialogue, interiority, and sensory anchor merely
for checklist completeness.

Honor the content intensity declared in §15 Plan Frontmatter. Do not invent
facts beyond the state context supplied in the plan. Do not resolve any mystery
declared forbidden in §11 Forbidden Mystery Resolutions. Respect the "Prose must
not imply" and "Anti-generic warnings" material in §16a STCHAR packets, plus the
Anti-Pathology Checklist at §18. Do not use internal record-identifier
vocabulary in the prose.

Honor the Prose Craft Contract above. Keep POV discipline, cut filter words, put
action in the verb, anchor abstraction to concrete sensory specifics, vary
anchors and metaphor stocks across pages, trust subtext, and use record-id-free
language throughout.

Output continuous prose only. No commentary. No markdown headers. No engine
vocabulary.
```
